# Umo Onboarding — Image Generation Prompts

Prompts for **Midjourney** or **Nano Banana**. Use these to generate hero graphics for the 3 onboarding slides. **Swiss design × Apple design** — minimal, infographic, composed of a few key elements. Images sit in the top ~60% of each slide.

---

## Brand context (for reference)

- **Umo**: Bill-splitting app for Zimbabwe & region. Split the bill, not the mood.
- **Palette**: Hot pink `#FF0048` accent, deep blacks `#111111` / `#050505`, clean whites.
- **Vibe**: Fintech × nightlife — dark, high-contrast, editorial. Premium but approachable.

---

## Style lock (use across all 3)

**Design philosophy**: Swiss design meets Apple — minimal, geometric, grid-based. Real objects (plates, glasses, money, hands, phones) arranged in a composed, refined layout. Lots of negative space. No clutter.

**Palette**: Deep blacks (#111111, #050505), clean whites, hot pink (#FF0048) accent — used sparingly for one focal element.

```
Swiss design, Apple design philosophy. Real objects — restaurant plates, glasses, food, money, hands, phones — arranged in a minimal composed layout. Grid-based, generous negative space. Color palette: deep blacks, clean whites, hot pink accent sparingly. Refined, premium --ar 1:1 --v 6
```

---

## Prompt 1 — ITEMIZE  
*"Every item, accounted for."*

```
Real objects arranged in a minimal Swiss-style composition. A restaurant table surface — wooden or white — with 4–5 items placed in a clean grid: a wine glass, a plate with food, a cocktail glass, a small bowl. Each item spaced apart, like a product shot. A hand or two visible — fingers pointing or resting near an item, suggesting "this one's mine." Maybe a receipt or phone at the edge. One item or label highlighted with hot pink accent. Deep blacks, clean whites, generous negative space. Swiss design, Apple design philosophy. Real plates, real glasses, real food — but composed, not messy --ar 1:1 --v 6
```

---

## Prompt 2 — CONVERT  
*"USD. ZiG. Your call."*

```
Real objects arranged in a minimal Swiss-style composition. A restaurant table with money and a phone. US dollar bills on one side, local banknotes (Zimbabwean) on the other — fanned or stacked neatly. A phone screen visible between them, showing an exchange rate or converter. A hand reaching toward the phone or resting on the notes. Empty glasses or a plate in the background, pushed aside. Objects placed in a clean grid — not scattered. One element (the phone glow, the rate number, or an arrow) in hot pink accent. Deep blacks, clean whites, generous negative space. Swiss design, Apple design philosophy. Real money, real phone, real hands — composed --ar 1:1 --v 6
```

---

## Prompt 3 — SETTLE  
*"Close the loop instantly."*

```
Real objects arranged in a minimal Swiss-style composition. End of a meal — a mostly cleared table. One hand extending across the table, offering cash to another. The receiving hand open, relaxed. Or: a phone held up, thumb on screen — the one-tap moment. A receipt or bill visible, maybe a checkmark drawn on it. Empty glasses, a coaster. Objects placed in a clean grid. The gesture of exchange is the focus — hand to hand, or hand to phone. One element (the cash, the phone screen, the checkmark) in hot pink accent. Deep blacks, clean whites, generous negative space. Swiss design, Apple design philosophy. Real hands, real money, real restaurant — composed --ar 1:1 --v 6
```

---

## Usage tips

- **Aspect ratio**: `--ar 1:1` (square).
- **Consistency**: Each composition uses the same design language — Swiss × Apple, 3–5 elements, one hot pink accent, lots of space.
- **Negative prompt** (if supported): `--no faces, photography, illustration, sketch, clutter, decorative, colorful, busy`
